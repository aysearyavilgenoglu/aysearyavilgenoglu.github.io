Video Game Sales Analysis

Interactive insights built from videogames_wide.csv

1. Genre × Platform Heatmap

Global sales intensity across different segments.

Insight: Action games on PS3 and Sports games on Wii show the highest global sales density. This suggests that certain genres are highly platform-dependent for their success.

2. Sales Over Time by Genre

Tracking global trends through stacked area analysis.



Insight: The industry saw a massive boom between 2005 and 2010. While Sports games were dominant early on, Action games took over the lead in total global sales after 2008.

3. Regional Sales by Platform

Distribution of sales across major global markets.

Insight: While North America and Europe show similar platform preferences, Japan has a unique market where handheld platforms like DS and 3DS represent a much larger share of the total sales compared to Xbox or PC.

4. Top 15 Publishers

Ranking industry leaders by total global revenue.


Insight: Nintendo leads the global market by a significant margin, primarily due to its strong first-party titles. The gap between the top publisher and others highlights the impact of exclusive franchises on global revenue.
